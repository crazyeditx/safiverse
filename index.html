How It Works

My Design Process —
Simple, Clear, Delivered.

Every project follows the same structured process so you always know what to expect and when to expect it.

01
Discovery
Brief & Brand Understanding
We start with a detailed brief covering your product, target audience, competitors, and the feeling you want your brand to give off. The more I know, the better the design.
02
Concept
Research & Initial Concepts
I research your market, study the competition, and develop initial design directions. This is where the creative thinking happens before any pixel gets placed.
03
Design
Build & Refine in Illustrator
I design in Adobe Illustrator, building layouts, choosing typography, refining colors until everything looks sharp, consistent, and on-brand. You get a preview to review.
04
Revisions
Your Feedback, Applied Fast
You review the design and share feedback. I apply revisions until you are completely happy with the result. No rushed delivery. We get it right.
05
Delivery
Print-Ready Files, Yours to Keep
Final files delivered in AI, PDF, and PNG formats. CMYK color mode, proper bleed and trim marks, ready to send straight to your printer or manufacturer.
